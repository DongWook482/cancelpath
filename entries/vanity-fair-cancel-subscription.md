## Why Users Get Stuck

Vanity Fair's official FAQ says you can cancel inside your online account page. In practice, subscribers report a loop between "signed in" and "please sign in":

> "When I click to look at my account it keeps asking for my login details?? So always back to square one." — `@ms_semw` on X (Nov 23, 2026), a digital subscriber who said they did not have enough details to cancel.

The same pattern shows up on another Condé Nast title. A Wired digital subscriber on `r/darkpatterns` described being signed in and able to read articles, but clicking **Manage subscription** asked them to sign in again; password reset said the email wasn't registered, while registering said it already was. They report finally sorting it out **through several emails to customer support**.

Vanity Fair's FAQ explains one reason this happens: *it is possible to create an online account without being a subscriber, and vice versa*, so a subscription may need to be **linked** to your online account before the account page shows it.

## Step 1: Try the Online Account Page

1. Sign in at [vanityfair.com/account/profile](https://www.vanityfair.com/account/profile) using the **email tied to your subscription** (for PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay purchases, the FAQ says the wallet's email becomes your account username).
2. Look for your subscription and the cancel option. The official FAQ: "You can cancel your subscription within your online account page."
3. If you forgot your password: click **Sign In → Forgot your password, reset it** and follow the email link (official FAQ steps).

If the page shows no subscription or keeps bouncing you back to sign-in, don't keep looping. Go to Step 2.

## Step 2: Use the Condé Nast Customer Care Routes (Print or Linked Subscriptions)

The official Vanity Fair subscription FAQ hosted by Condé Nast's fulfillment site lists these cancellation channels:

- **Online cancel link:** on the [Vanity Fair Subscriptions FAQ page](https://w1.buysub.com/pubs/N3/VYF/VYF_FAQ.jsp?cds_mag_code=VYF&cds_page_id=37915&lsid=90121131409021321&vid=1), under "Cancellation."
- **Email:** VYFcustserv@cdsfulfillment.com
- **Phone:** 1-800-365-0635
- **Mail:** Vanity Fair, PO Box 37714, Boone, IA 50037-0714

That page says they will "promptly stop your service and issue a refund for any unmailed copies," and that bills arriving a few days after cancelling can be ignored while the cancellation takes effect.

**Tip:** Email gives you a written record. Include your full name, the email on the account, your mailing address (or account number from a mailing label/invoice), and a clear "please cancel my subscription and send written confirmation."

## Step 3: Use Live Chat for Digital / Account-Linking Problems

Vanity Fair's [Contact Us page](https://www.vanityfair.com/contact/contact-us) says the fastest way to reach Customer Care is the **live chat widget in the bottom-right corner** of that page. Use it to:

- Link your subscription to your online account (the FAQ says to use Contact Us if linking by account number or mailing address fails).
- Merge duplicate accounts (the FAQ says staff can combine them).
- Ask for **written confirmation of cancellation**. The FAQ says confirmation is sent on request via the Contact Us page.

## Step 4: Subscribed Inside the Apple App? Cancel With Apple

If you bought through the Vanity Fair app on iPhone/iPad, Apple bills you. Per [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428): **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → select the subscription → Cancel Subscription**. If there's no Cancel button or you see an expiration message in red, it's already canceled.

## What Happens After You Cancel

Per the official Vanity Fair FAQ:

- **No refunds** for cancellations on vanityfair.com subscriptions; the subscription stays active through the end of the current billing term and you won't be charged again.
- **Print:** cancellation is processed immediately, but you may receive **one to two more issues**.
- **Digital:** you keep access until the end of your existing term, which won't renew.
- **Keep print, drop digital (or vice versa)?** The FAQ says to submit that request through the Contact Us page.

## Not Something You Ordered?

Redditors in `r/Scams` report receiving Vanity Fair issues they never ordered and being told by Vanity Fair that the subscription came through a **third-party vendor**, which had to be contacted directly. If the charge on your statement isn't from Condé Nast/Vanity Fair, cancel with the seller named on the charge.

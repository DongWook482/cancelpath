How to Cancel a Patreon Membership (2026): Web, App, Apple iOS Billing & Removing Your Card

Can't find the cancel button, still charged after cancelling, or want your card off Patreon? Cancel per creator in Settings → Memberships (web) or the creator page (app), cancel Apple-billed memberships in iPhone Settings, check for a second account, then remove the card or delete the account.

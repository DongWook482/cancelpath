How to Cancel LinkedIn Premium (2026): Desktop-Only Trap, Apple/Google Play Routes & Billing Cutoff

Updated: 2026-11-24 · Method: LinkedIn desktop website / LinkedIn Android app / LinkedIn iOS app (App Store purchases) / Google Play / Apple Subscriptions

Billing Cutoff Rule
Cancel at least one day before the next billing date. Paid plans stay active until the end of the current billing cycle; cancelling a free trial ends Premium immediately.
Quick takeaway: Why the LinkedIn iPhone app often shows no cancel option, which device you must use depending on where you bought Premium (LinkedIn web, Apple App Store or Google Play), the exact menu path from LinkedIn Help, and the one-day billing cutoff.

Why Users Get Stuck

LinkedIn ties the cancel button to where you originally paid, not to the device you're holding. That is the source of most "there is no cancel button" complaints.

"This is crazy, trying to cancel my subscription. Tried numerous methods and will not work." — @robjohnsonrj1 replying to @LinkedIn on X (Nov 24, 2026)

On r/LegalAdviceUK, a job seeker who accepted a free Premium month in the mobile app tried to cancel shortly afterwards and found no way to do it on mobile; they say LinkedIn staff confirmed the only way was a desktop computer, which they couldn't reach while travelling.

LinkedIn's own FAQ confirms the rule: if you signed up directly through LinkedIn (desktop or mobile web), you can only cancel from the LinkedIn desktop website or the LinkedIn Android app. If you bought on an iPhone through the mobile web, LinkedIn processes the payment and *cancellation isn't available in the iOS app* — you must use desktop.

Step 0: Work Out How You Paid

  • Bought on linkedin.com (desktop or mobile browser, including Safari on iPhone): cancel on the LinkedIn desktop website or in the LinkedIn Android app.
  • Bought inside the LinkedIn iPhone/iPad app (Apple App Store billing): cancel in the LinkedIn iOS app or in Apple's subscription settings.
  • Bought inside the LinkedIn Android app (Google Play billing): cancel on the LinkedIn desktop site, in the LinkedIn mobile app, or in Google Play (web or app).

Tip: check your card statement or email receipts. An Apple or Google Play receipt means the app store bills you; a LinkedIn receipt means LinkedIn does.

Step 1: Bought Directly from LinkedIn → Use a Desktop Browser

LinkedIn Help (Cancel LinkedIn Premium subscription) says you can go straight to the cancellation page from that article. The menu path LinkedIn documents on desktop is:

  1. Click the Me icon at the top of your LinkedIn homepage.
  2. Select Premium features.
  3. Open your subscription details / Manage subscription (you are redirected to the LinkedIn Admin Center).
  4. Click Purchases in the left pane.
  5. Select your Active subscription.
  6. Click Cancel subscription under Actions, then Confirm cancel.

No computer? LinkedIn's FAQ says the Android app also works for direct purchases. The FAQ does not offer an iOS-app route for web purchases.

Step 2: Bought in the iPhone App (Apple App Store)

From LinkedIn Help:

  1. In the LinkedIn iOS app, tap your profile photo.
  2. Tap Premium features → Manage your subscription.
  3. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the prompts.

You can also cancel through Apple's subscription settings (Apple Support). LinkedIn says it can't provide billing support for App Store purchases: payment questions and refund requests go to Apple.

Step 3: Bought in the Android App (Google Play)

From LinkedIn Help, you can cancel on the LinkedIn desktop site (Me → Premium features → Subscription details → Purchases → Active subscription → Cancel subscription → Confirm cancel), in the LinkedIn app (profile photo → Premium features → Manage your subscription → Cancel subscription), or in Google Play (Google Play Help). LinkedIn can't show purchase history for Google Play subscriptions; check your Google order history instead.

Deadlines and What Happens After

  • Cancel at least one day before your next billing date to avoid another charge.
  • A paid plan stays active until the end of the current billing cycle, then drops to Basic (free).
  • A free trial that you cancel ends immediately, along with Premium access.
  • Accumulated InMail credits are lost and can't be credited back.
  • You can't delete your payment method while a paid subscription is active; you can remove it after the billing cycle ends.
  • To close your LinkedIn account, you must cancel the subscription first.
  • If you only want to pay less, LinkedIn offers downgrading to another Premium plan, on desktop only.

If It Still Won't Cancel

  • Confirm you're signed in to the same LinkedIn account that holds the subscription.
  • For direct-billing problems, LinkedIn says to contact LinkedIn customer support (linked from the Help article). For Apple or Google Play billing, contact that store.
  • Missed a trial cutoff? LinkedIn Help points to its refund policy from the cancellation article.

About CancelPath

CancelPath is an independent, free directory of verified routes for canceling subscriptions and memberships. Official help pages, billing portals, and mobile apps often hide, relocate, or break the cancel button. We publish one practical, source-cited guide at a time so people can find the working path before the next charge hits.

How guides are written

  • Each page starts from a real, recent complaint (public posts on X or Reddit) and is checked against the company's official support or terms page.
  • We do not invent URLs, cutoff dates, phone numbers, or quotes. Every claim is cited.
  • CancelPath is not affiliated with Planet Fitness, WHOOP, Oura, Apple, Google, or any other service named in a guide.

Project status

The site is a static directory published at dongwook482.github.io/cancelpath from the public repository github.com/DongWook482/cancelpath. No user accounts, comments, or payments are collected here.

Advertising and affiliate links are not active yet. Reserved slots exist in the page layout for a future approved ad unit; they stay empty until a network is connected and disclosed.

We use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

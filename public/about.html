About CancelPath

Last updated: 27 November 2026.

CancelPath is an independent, free directory of verified routes for canceling subscriptions and memberships. Official help pages, billing portals, and mobile apps often hide, relocate, or break the cancel button. We publish one practical, source-cited guide at a time so people can find the working path before the next charge hits.

How guides are researched

Each page starts from real, recent public complaints — typically posts on X or Reddit describing a failed cancel, a surprise renewal, or a billing-channel mismatch. Those reports are then cross-checked against the company's official help pages or account portals and, when a claim involves consumer rights, against regulator materials (for example CFPB or FTC guidance, or a cited state cancellation rule). We do not invent URLs, cutoff dates, phone numbers, quotes, or legal conclusions. Every factual claim is cited on the page.

Editorial independence

  • CancelPath is not affiliated with Planet Fitness, WHOOP, Oura, Apple, Google, Uber, or any other service named in a guide.
  • Editorial choices are independent of advertisers. Advertising and affiliate links are not active yet. Reserved slots exist in the page layout for a future approved ad unit; they stay empty until a network is connected and disclosed.
  • No user accounts, comments, or payments are collected on this site.

Not legal advice

CancelPath guides are general information about cancellation routes and publicly available consumer-protection materials. They are not legal advice, and they are not a substitute for advice from a qualified attorney or from the company or regulator that issued a given rule. Always confirm cancellation in writing and check your next statement.
